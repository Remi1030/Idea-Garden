{Idea Garden}

(Overview): Idea Garden is a web app that encourages anyone to explore many different ideas, as well as organize them in a common space for future purposes. Many students or busy individuals struggle with sorting their notes into a proper folder or an online document, and this can lead to management issues and important information getting lost in other categories. Whether you're planning a birthday party or drawing mind maps for a biology exam, Idea Garden has many different uses for anybody who uses it. 

(Functions): Idea Garden has three main features: sticky notes, stationery, and tabs. 

1. Sticky notes are draggable and can have text on them to jot down all your notes.
2. Stationery can include pencils, pens, highlighters, erasers, & markers, and they can be used for drawing pictures and making your board aesthetically pleasing.
3. Tabs are similar to browsers, and they hold a separate whiteboard for each scenario, so that you don't have to work on one page!

This app is different from a Notes app because it uses movable sticky notes, which are much easier to visualize than lines of text and information. It also encourages creativity in users because it features many colors that allow the user to draw out their idea and display it on their whiteboard!

Instructions: To use the sticky note function, press the script icon button to your left. All of the stationery items are in the vertical toolbelt at the left side of the page, and the rest of the space is the main workspace, called the whiteboard. If you need to add a tab, click the "+" sign at the top, rename your browser to a relevant name for your current use, and you get started on a new tab now!

Main Troubleshooting Challenges: My main challenge was the layout of the page because I went through many different layouts until I decided on a main whiteboard with a side toolbelt kit. At first, I started with a drawpad layout, but the stationery took up too much space. To solve this, I changed the layout into a side toolbelt that uses icon buttons to represent the tool. Additionally, I ran into an issue with the mobility of the sticky notes. The notes had glitchy movement, and it caused the program to often crash, but with some troubleshooting, I was able to fix the error!

TechStack: I used HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for my program. Additionally, I used Codex Linux as a development assistant to help me brainstorm improvements. However, the troubleshooting, editing, and creation process were all done by me. I programmed this app.
